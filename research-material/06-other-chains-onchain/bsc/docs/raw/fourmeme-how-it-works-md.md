# How it works?

1. **Configure**

Begin by setting up all the necessary information for your meme token. This includes defining the token name, symbol, and other key details. Four.meme launchpad provides a user-friendly interface to help you configure these settings efficiently.

Launching your project on Four.meme requires no technical skills, takes only a few seconds.

> *Note: The preset for all Memecoin's Total Supply launched through Four.meme Launchpad is 1,000,000,000.*

* Token Name: \[Your coin name]
* Ticker Symbol: \*\*\*\*\[Your coin symbol]
* Description: \[A short description about your project]
* Raised Token: \[Types of token used for trading and liquidity]
* Logo: \[Your logo]
* Socials: \[Your socials for your community to engage and stay up to date]

**Multi-Token Trading**

With multi-token trading feature, when you create a token on Four.meme, you can now choose one of the supported tokens—$BNB, $USDT, $USD1, $UUSD or bStocks — as the trading pair in the internal liquidity pool. Once the pool reaches 100%, 20% of your tokens and all the collected supported tokens will be paired on PancakeSwap to establish liquidity.

1. Launch
2. Before launching, developers have the opportunity to purchase a portion of their token supply in the same transaction to protect your coins from snipers.
3. Lastly, confirm the transaction, and you're all set to go.

> *Note: Launching your project is free of charge on Four.meme, the only fee you will pay is the transaction fee which is \~0.005 BNB*

#### **Service fees**

Fair launching your project is virtually cost-free and immediately tradable, eliminating the need for seeding initial liquidity.

* Launching Fee

You'll only have to cover the transaction fee, which is around \~**0.005 BNB.**

* Trading Fee

While trading on our platform we take only 1% **fee**（minimum is 0.001 BNB）

* Seeding Fee

Once your project reaches 100% of the bonding curve (approximately 18 BNB), the seeding process will begin. We will establish a liquidity pool on Pancake paired with 18 BNB.
