# NodeReal Builder

# 

NodeReal Builder

### 

Introduction

**NodeReal Builder** is the leading block-building infrastructure provider in the **BNB Smart Chain (BSC)** ecosystem. It delivers secure and high-performance transaction routing services designed to protect users against **front-running** , **sandwich attacks** , and other MEV exploitation strategies.  
NodeReal operates as a member of the **Good Will Alliance** , a collective initiative formed by top infrastructure providers to ensure fairness, transparency, and user protection across blockchain networks.  
With its robust infrastructure and deep integration with the BSC validator set, NodeReal Builder is one of the most **critical building blocks** of the BSC ecosystem.

### 

Why NodeReal Builder?

  * **Trusted Infrastructure Provider** : Powers numerous applications, exchanges, and protocols on BNB Smart Chain.
  * **Transaction Security** : Submissions bypass the public mempool, eliminating exposure to malicious MEV strategies.
  * **Alliance-Backed Protection** : Good Will Alliance membership ensures fair execution and user safety.
  * **Optimized Performance** : Global infrastructure with minimal latency and fast propagation.



### 

Endpoint

All requests are submitted via JSON-RPC to:  
<https://bsc-mainnet-builder.nodereal.io>

### 

Security & Ecosystem Role

  * **MEV Protection** : Direct routing to validators without public mempool exposure.
  * **Censorship Resistance** : Fair inclusion of transactions across the validator landscape.
  * **Ecosystem Impact** : Backbone for dApps, wallets, and trading protocols on BSC.
