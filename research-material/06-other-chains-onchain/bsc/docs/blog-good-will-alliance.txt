# BNB Good Will Alliance

2025.3.18 • 1 min read

In response to the [_call to reduce malicious MEV attacks_](<https://snapshot.box/#/s:bnbchain-dao.eth/proposal/0x8f6bee39a3da4feeb0906f7bfb9132757781a3c009a4aa3ed230ec33f2de99b8>) and other community actions in good will to help with the BNB ecosystem's growth and development, the BNB Chain core community now unites several prominent members to launch the BNB Good Will Alliance.

**Purpose of the Good Will Alliance**

BNB Good Will Alliance is established to coordinate off-chain efforts to initiate creative ideas, best practices, and ethical standards that serve the best interests of BNB holders and all participants in the BNB ecosystem. These coordinations are expected to become a more widely accepted consensus via [_BNB governance_](<https://www.bnbchain.org/en/bnb-chain-governance>), [_BEPs_](<https://github.com/bnb-chain/BEPs>), and BNB Chain code.

The first initiative of the alliance is to aggressively push for the reduction of Sandwich Attacks on BNB Smart Chain (BSC). The two key builders in BNB Chain’s infrastructure, [_BlockRazor_](<https://x.com/BlockRazor_Inc/status/1901860873016336438>), and [_48 Club_](<https://x.com/48Club_Official/status/1901656505071591641>), have now deployed their Sandwich Attack filters onto their block building mechanism. These builders now form the first batch of Builders in this initiative. To respond to this good will action, we call for all BSC builders to join this initiative to filter Sandwich Attacks with their best efforts. 

**Call For Validators  **

We have created a repository on GitHub for the alliance, with MEV good will builders in [_a dedicated folder_](<https://github.com/bnb-chain/good-will-alliance/tree/main/mev-info/bsc-mainnet/builders>). We call for all BSC validators to now only accept block bids from builders included in this folder.

**Next Steps**

We will work with the members of the alliance to perform further actions to resist Sandwich Attacks, including:

  1. Define a quality concept of Sandwich Attacks
  2. Create toolings to recognize Sandwich Attacks as a measure of the performance of builders and validators
  3. Introduce community collaborations to create more infrastructure to share how to detect and prevent Sandwich Attacks



In the near future, we will work with more alliance members to introduce more good will initiatives to bring more benefits to the community. Stay tuned for further updates!
